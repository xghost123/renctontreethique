🔍 TROUBLESHOOTING:

Issue: "SQLite driver not found"
Fix:   Always use: php -c php.ini.dev [command]
       Or use: ./dev.sh [command]

Issue: Port 8000 already in use
Fix:   Use different port: php -c php.ini.dev artisan serve --port=8001

Issue: "SQLSTATE[HY000]: General error"
Fix:   Reset database: rm database/database.sqlite
       Then: ./dev.sh migrate
